🔒 How your data is handled — please read this

Your file never leaves this browser tab. The spreadsheet you upload is read and processed entirely on your own device — it is never uploaded to any server, by Deep Pocket or anyone else.

What we DO use AI for: matching your column headers (e.g. "Client Name", "Mob No") to the right field in Deep Pocket CRM. To do this, we send only:

  • Your column header text (the labels you chose — not client data)
  • An anonymized statistical summary of each column — for example, "78% filled, average 10 characters, 92% match a 10-digit mobile number pattern." This describes the shape of the data, never the data itself.

What we never send, ever: no client name, phone number, PAN, email, date of birth, or any other actual value from your file is transmitted to any AI service, at any point in this process.

Nothing is stored. This AI request is stateless — it isn't logged, saved, or kept anywhere after it responds with a suggested mapping. It won't be reused, reviewed, or referenced again in the future, by us or anyone else.

The suggested mapping is only a starting point — please review and correct it yourself before downloading, especially for sensitive fields like PAN, mobile, and financial figures. You remain responsible for confirming the final mapping is correct.
