1. Introduction

Wealth Matrix Finserv LLP ("we", "us", "our", "the Firm") provides wealth management and financial advisory services. This Policy explains what personal data we collect from you, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it, in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, together with applicable regulations of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) governing client records.

This Policy applies to all individuals whose personal data we collect — leads, prospective clients, existing clients, and their family members or nominees where relevant — through our advisory relationship, our website/application, and any document-upload or information-gathering links we send you.

2. Key Terms

  • Data Principal — the individual to whom the personal data relates (you).
  • Data Fiduciary — the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data (Wealth Matrix Finserv LLP, in this relationship).
  • Personal Data — any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  • Processing — any operation on personal data, including collection, storage, use, sharing, and erasure.
  • Consent Manager — a person registered with the Data Protection Board of India who enables a Data Principal to give, manage, review, or withdraw consent (not currently used by the Firm; consent is managed directly).

3. What This Notice Covers (Section 5 Disclosure)

Statutory notice — Section 5, DPDP Act 2023

Before we collect and process your personal data, this notice discloses, in plain language: (a) the personal data being collected and its itemized description (Section 4), (b) the specific purpose for which it is processed (Section 5), (c) the manner in which you may exercise your rights under Section 12 (access, correction, erasure) (Section 11), (d) the manner in which you may make a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India (Section 14), and (e) how to withdraw your consent, which must be as easy as giving it (Section 6).

5. Why We Collect It

We process your personal data only for the following specific purposes:

  • To assess your suitability for, and provide, wealth management and financial advisory services.
  • To complete KYC (Know Your Customer) and onboarding as required under applicable law.
  • To communicate with you regarding your investments, follow-ups, and servicing requests.
  • To generate portfolio insights and review your existing holdings and protection coverage, at your request.
  • To meet our recordkeeping obligations under SEBI/AMFI regulations.
  • To send you service-related communication, including via WhatsApp, at your registered mobile number.

We do not use your personal data for any purpose beyond what is disclosed here, and we do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Your Consent

We collect your personal data only with your free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent, given through a clear affirmative action (such as ticking a consent checkbox before submitting a form). We do not use pre-ticked boxes, bundled consent, or silence as a substitute for consent. Where a form on our site or app collects both mandatory information (needed for the specific service) and optional information, we will indicate clearly which is which.

7. Use of AI-Assisted Tools

To help our team prepare for client meetings, review portfolios, and draft communications more efficiently, we use AI-assisted tools (currently, Anthropic's Claude). We disclose specifically how this applies to the personal data we collect:

  • Portfolio and CAS review. When reviewing your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) or similar mutual fund holding statements, our system uses your PAN — already printed on that statement — solely to correctly identify and isolate your own holdings from the document, since a single CAS can sometimes contain another family member's holdings if you share a registered email with them. Your PAN is used for this internal matching purpose only, and our tools are configured not to reproduce it in full within any summary or output generated for our team's review.
  • Meeting preparation and message drafting. Where we use AI assistance to prepare a briefing ahead of a client meeting, or to draft a WhatsApp message on our behalf, relevant details from your profile (such as your name, holdings summary, and notes on file) may be shared with this tool for that specific purpose.

In every case, data shared with this tool is used solely to generate the requested output for our team's use, and is not used by the provider to train any AI model. Because this processor is based outside India, this involves a transfer of your data outside India for processing. Under the DPDP Act, such transfers are currently permitted by default, except to any country the Government of India specifically restricts; no such restriction currently applies here. We take reasonable steps to minimize what is shared for any AI-assisted purpose beyond what each specific task requires.

8. Who We Share Data With

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • With Registrars and Transfer Agents (such as CAMS, KFintech) and Asset Management Companies, strictly as required to process your investment transactions.
  • With our AI processing provider, as described in Section 7, solely for the purposes described.
  • Where required by law, regulation, or a valid order of a court, tribunal, or regulatory authority (including SEBI).
  • With your explicit consent, for any other specific purpose.

9. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your personal data for as long as our advisory relationship continues, and thereafter for the period required under applicable SEBI/AMFI recordkeeping obligations. Where you were a lead who did not convert to a client, or where you have withdrawn consent, we will retain only what is necessary for legitimate recordkeeping or legal purposes, and will review such data periodically for deletion where no longer required.

10. How We Protect Your Data

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss, including access controls on our internal systems, secure storage of uploaded documents, and restricting access to your data to personnel who need it to service your account. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required under the DPDP Act.

11. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities we have carried out on it.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Erase personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to our legal recordkeeping obligations.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal, as described in Section 13 below.

12. Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time, with effect going forward, by contacting us using the details in Section 13. Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and may affect our ability to continue providing certain services to you. Withdrawal is as simple as giving consent — a message to us is sufficient; no additional justification is required.

15. Children's and Persons with Disabilities' Data

Where we process personal data relating to a nominee or family member who is a minor or a person with a disability, we do so only with the verifiable consent of their parent or lawful guardian, and we do not use such data for behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising.
